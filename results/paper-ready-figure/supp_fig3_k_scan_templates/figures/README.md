### supp_fig3_k_scan_and_multipatient_templates.png

**Supplementary Fig. 3 | Model-order scanning identifies two temporal templates in most patients while retaining higher-order solutions.**

**A,** Unsupervised clustering was evaluated for K = 2–10 using event-by-contact recruitment-rank features after masking non-participating contacts in 40 patients; for each patient, K was selected as the solution with the highest median silhouette among those with median adjusted mutual information (AMI) ≥ 0.70 across seeds and a smallest-cluster fraction ≥ 0.10. The left panel shows the selected-K distribution (34/40 patients selected K = 2), the middle panel shows each patient's silhouette difference relative to K = 2 with the cohort median and interquartile range, and the right panel shows the percentage of patients passing both gates at each K. **B,** Recruitment-order maps for two K = 4 solutions (E11 and E19) and one K = 6 solution (Y10), with 2,500 displayed population events per patient; colour denotes relative within-event recruitment order from first to last, light grey denotes a non-participating contact and hatched gaps separate templates. Profiles at right show the contact-wise mean rank for each template, with shading indicating ±1 s.d.

**关注点**：B 说明 K=2 是队列主导结构但不是先验强制结果；队列结论仍由上排全部 40 名患者的扫描承担。
