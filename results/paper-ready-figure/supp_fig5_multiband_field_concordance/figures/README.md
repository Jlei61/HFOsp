### supp_fig5_multiband_cohort_and_subject_heatmap.png

**Supplementary Fig. 5 | Multiband ictal concordance with Timing + 3D direction templates.**

**A,** Patient-level field-concordance difference (Δ) across seven frequency bands in 17 patients. For each patient and band, D is the median early-ictal dense-grid spatial concordance with the better-matching TA or TB field defined by Timing + 3D direction clustering after resolving sign and transverse-mirror symmetry, and Δ is D minus the median of that patient's all-contact permutation null (1000 draws); points denote patients, violins the distributions and black horizontal lines the cohort medians. Red violins and stars denote a coherent cohort spatial-null test within that frequency band (one-sided permutation P < 0.05, uncorrected across the seven bands). **B,** Heat map of the same patient-by-band Δ values, with rows ordered by the number of bands exceeding the patient's own null and the bottom row showing cohort medians; outlined stars denote one-sided empirical patient-versus-own-null P < 0.05 and are not cohort-level or multiplicity-corrected. Bands are δ (1–4 Hz), θ (4–8 Hz), α (8–13 Hz), β (13–30 Hz), γ (30–80 Hz), R (80–150 Hz) and FR (150–250 Hz). Five bands passed the per-band spatial-null test, whereas no band passed the seven-band maxT correction (minimum P_FWER = 0.058); the direct between-band omnibus test was also not significant (calibrated P = 0.522).

**关注点**：该版本使用 Timing + 3D direction TA/TB 模板。A 中 A 的星号表示逐频带 cohort spatial-null P<0.05，未作七频带校正；0 个频带通过七频带 maxT-FWER。B 的 cell 星号仅表示患者自身 null P<0.05，不是队列水平结果。频带间直接 omnibus 检验为非显著，不能声称存在优势频带。
