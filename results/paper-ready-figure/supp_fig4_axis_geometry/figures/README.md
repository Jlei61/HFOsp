### supp_fig4_axis_direction_and_heldout_readback.png

**Supplementary Fig. 4 | Spatial information strengthens held-out direction read-out of interictal recruitment axes.**

**A,** Half-rose distribution of the directed angular separation between the spatial axes associated with paired interictal temporal templates TA and TB across 28 patients, where 0° denotes the same direction and 180° the opposite direction; sector labels give patient counts in 30° bins, the radial black line marks the median and the outer black arc the interquartile range (IQR). **B,** Paired patient-level direction scores for timing-only and timing-plus-space clustering, evaluated on held-out recording blocks. The score is the equal-fold, equal-template mean signed cosine between each held-out event direction and its assigned training-template axis. Points denote patients, lines preserve patient pairing, violins show the distributions, boxes show IQRs and black horizontal lines mark medians. The median score increased from 0.506 to 0.568; 21 of 25 patients improved, with a median paired gain of 0.028 (95% bootstrap CI, 0.001–0.063; one-sided paired Wilcoxon P = 0.000908). **C,** Patient-level Spearman correlation between held-out contact recruitment rank and the path-axis coordinate fitted using the complementary event half in Yuquan (n = 7 patients) and Epilepsiae (n = 19 patients). Points denote patients (blue, Yuquan; purple, Epilepsiae), violins show the distributions, horizontal black lines mark medians and vertical black lines mark IQRs. Across both cohorts, the median held-out ρ was 0.752 (IQR, 0.500–0.842; n = 26 patients). **D,** Clinical SOZ-contact compactness relative to each patient's mapped invasive-contact geometry. The SOZ RMS radius was divided by the median radius of 20,000 equally sized random contact subsets from the same patient; the dashed line marks the null ratio of 1. Points denote patients (blue, Yuquan, n = 15; purple, Epilepsiae, n = 14), filled points indicate a patient-level empirical P < 0.05, and horizontal and vertical black lines mark medians and IQRs. Brackets show one-sided within-dataset Wilcoxon signed-rank tests of log2 ratios against 0 (Yuquan, P = 3.1e-05; Epilepsiae, P = 0.00012; *** denotes P < 0.001). Across both cohorts, the median ratio was 0.375 (95% bootstrap CI, 0.261–0.478; n = 29 patients; one-sided Wilcoxon P = 3.7e-09).

**关注点**：B 显示真实空间信息带来的留出方向增益：21/25 名患者提高，中位增益 0.028（95% CI 0.001–0.063）。C 共 n=26，中位 ρ=0.752，IQR 0.500–0.842。D 共 n=29，SOZ/null RMS 半径比中位数=0.375，95% CI 0.261–0.478。D 只支持 clinical SOZ 触点相对患者自身植入几何更紧凑，不证明群体事件局限于 SOZ。已进入主图的 2D field panel 不在补图重复；完整患者数值进入补充表。
