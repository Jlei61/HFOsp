**Supplementary Fig. 4 | Three-dimensional direction shifts paired template axes and improves held-out directional organization.**

**A,** Paired half-rose distributions of the directed angular separation between the full-fit TA and TB axes obtained using Timing-only (blue) and Timing + 3D direction (orange) clustering in 28 patients. 0° denotes the same direction and 180° the opposite direction; overlapping bars show the fraction of patients in 30° bins, with the blended region denoting the shared fraction. Coloured diamonds mark means and outer arcs show mean ± 1 s.d. The mean separation was 101.3° ± 45.0° for Timing-only and 134.6° ± 34.4° for Timing + 3D direction. **B,** Paired patient-level direction scores for Timing-only and Timing + 3D direction clustering, evaluated by alternating-recording-block cross-validation. All interictal events contributed timing features, whereas events with finite direction estimates additionally contributed the masked spatial view. The score is the equal-fold, equal-template mean signed cosine between each held-out event direction and its assigned training-template axis. Points denote patients, lines preserve patient pairing, violins show the distributions, boxes show IQRs and black horizontal lines mark medians. The median score increased from 0.435 to 0.479; 22 of 26 patients improved, with a median paired gain of 0.042 (95% bootstrap CI, 0.011–0.064; one-sided paired Wilcoxon P = 9.54e-06). **C,** The same held-out direction scores compared with the patient-specific median of each method's direction-shuffle null. Open and filled distributions denote null and observed scores, respectively; grey lines preserve within-patient pairing. Timing-only and Timing + 3D direction scores both exceeded their respective nulls (26 of 26 patients for each method; one-sided paired Wilcoxon P = 1.49e-08 and 1.49e-08, respectively). The upper bracket compares the two observed methods (one-sided paired Wilcoxon P = 9.54e-06). **D,** Clinical SOZ-contact compactness relative to each patient's mapped invasive-contact geometry. The SOZ RMS radius was divided by the median radius of 20,000 equally sized random contact subsets from the same patient; the dashed line marks the null ratio of 1. Points denote patients (blue, Yuquan, n = 15; purple, Epilepsiae, n = 14), filled points indicate a patient-level empirical P < 0.05, and horizontal and vertical black lines mark medians and IQRs. Brackets show one-sided within-dataset Wilcoxon signed-rank tests of log2 ratios against 0 (Yuquan, P = 3.1e-05; Epilepsiae, P = 0.00012; *** denotes P < 0.001). Across both cohorts, the median ratio was 0.375 (95% bootstrap CI, 0.261–0.478; n = 29 patients; one-sided Wilcoxon P = 3.7e-09).

**关注点**：A 直接比较两种聚类的 TA/TB 轴夹角分布，均值 ± s.d. 由 101.3° ± 45.0° 移至 134.6° ± 34.4°。B 显示真实三维方向信息带来的留出方向增益：22/26 名患者提高，中位增益 0.042（95% CI 0.011–0.064）。C 显示两种方法均在 26/26 名患者中超过各自的方向置换 null，并单独给出两种观测方法间的配对比较。A–C 沿用主图的 Timing-only / Timing + 3D direction 名称及蓝/橙语义色。D 共 n=29，SOZ/null RMS 半径比中位数=0.375，95% CI 0.261–0.478。D 只支持 clinical SOZ 触点相对患者自身植入几何更紧凑，不证明群体事件局限于 SOZ。已进入主图的 2D field panel 不在补图重复；完整患者数值进入补充表。
