**Supplementary Fig. 2 | SOZ localization of raw and group-event-filtered interictal HFO burden.**

**A,** Subject-level receiver operating characteristic curves for distinguishing clinically labelled seizure onset zone (SOZ) from non-SOZ contacts using contact-wise group-event-filtered high-frequency oscillation (HFO) burden in the Yuquan (20 patients) and Epilepsiae (15 patients) cohorts; grey lines denote individual patients, coloured lines the cohort mean, shading the s.e.m., and inset points the patient-level area under the curve (AUC), with the black line indicating the mean. Mean AUC was 0.873 in Yuquan and 0.955 in Epilepsiae. **B,** Paired patient-level AUC before (Raw) and after restricting refined HFO detections to accepted population-event windows (Synchronized); bars and black horizontal lines indicate cohort means, and points joined by lines denote paired patients (Yuquan, 0.837 versus 0.873; Epilepsiae, 0.939 versus 0.955). Statistical significance was assessed using a two-sided paired Wilcoxon test; *P < 0.05; n.s., P ≥ 0.05.

**关注点**：Yuquan 配对 n=20，mean AUC 0.837→0.873，双侧 Wilcoxon P=0.0108；Epilepsiae 配对 n=15，mean AUC 0.939→0.955，P=0.133。该图支持空间定位增益，不证明传播机制。
