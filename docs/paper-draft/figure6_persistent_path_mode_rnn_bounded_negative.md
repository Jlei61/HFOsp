## 一句话论证

The structured recurrent model learned local next-set statistics from
interictal contact-rank prefixes, but its free-running events did not recover
held-out contact-level participation and rank distributions better than simpler
controls, and the imposed path modes were not dynamically identifiable.

## Methods（copy-ready）

### Self-supervised generation of interictal contact-rank events

We tested whether the contact-level organization of interictal population
events could be generated by a recurrent process constrained by
patient-specific propagation paths. Masked contact-rank events from 34
patients were ordered chronologically and divided within patient into an 80%
training partition and a 20% held-out partition. Each event was represented as
a sequence of rank-tied contact sets followed by a stop action. Template
labels, inter-event intervals and seizure-derived measurements were excluded
from prior construction, training and model selection.

### Structured path-mode graph RNN

Patient-specific path modes were constructed exclusively from each training
partition. The formal model used two path modes, the smallest fixed structure
capable of expressing more than one propagation route. Each latent component
combined one path mode with one propagation direction and remained fixed until
event termination. A leaky state was defined over contacts and updated by the
observed contact set, the component-specific graph and a shared inhibitory
variable. Contact hazards combined the recurrent state with a
patient-specific one-dimensional offset, whereas stop probability was decoded
from event progress, excitation and inhibition. Component labels were not
assigned during training. Sequence likelihoods were marginalized over all
components, and component posteriors were updated using only the observed
event prefix.

### Leave-one-subject-out training and evaluation

For each held-out patient, shared recurrent parameters were fitted to the
training partitions of the other 33 patients. Shared training covered every
training patient twice, with eight optimizer updates per patient per coverage
cycle. Shared parameters were then frozen, and only the held-out patient's
local contact offset was calibrated on that patient's training partition for
four coverage cycles, again with eight updates per cycle. AdamW used learning
rates of \(10^{-3}\) for shared parameters and \(2\times10^{-3}\) for local
offsets, weight decay \(10^{-4}\), gradient clipping at 1.0 and batches of
1,024 events. Each fitted condition generated 5,000 free-running events.
Thirty-four held-out folds, three prespecified seeds and five model conditions
yielded 510 independent runs.

### Controls, lesions and formal gate

The intact two-mode model was compared with a model without event history, a
single merged path, within-mode edge-weight shuffling and mode shuffling that
preserved aggregate edge mass while disrupting coherent paths. Lesions of the
intact model removed the graph, inhibition, either propagation direction or
the dominant mode, or collapsed the two modes into one. The two primary
endpoints were participation MAE and per-contact rank-distribution
Wasserstein distance. Seed medians were first computed within patient.
Advancement required positive cohort-median benefit, improvement in at least
18 of 34 patients and one-sided patient-level Wilcoxon \(q<0.05\) after
Benjamini–Hochberg correction for both primary endpoints against every
control. Structural necessity additionally required either graph removal or
mode collapse to impair both endpoints under the same rule. Held-out next-set
NLL, pairwise precedence and whole-path distance were secondary diagnostics.
A prespecified sensitivity analysis excluded the three patients used during
model development.

## Results（copy-ready）

### Local prefix prediction did not translate into the target generative distributions

The intact model learned local conditional statistics under observed event
prefixes. Held-out next-set NLL was lower than the no-history control in all 34
patients (median benefit, 0.0203; \(q=5.8\times10^{-10}\)), the
edge-weight-shuffled control in all 34 patients (0.0137;
\(q=5.8\times10^{-10}\)), the single-path model in 29 of 34 patients (0.0020;
\(q=7.8\times10^{-5}\)) and the mode-shuffled control in 26 of 34 patients
(0.00020; \(q=0.016\)). These secondary likelihood gains did not generalize to
the prespecified free-running endpoints. The intact model improved
participation MAE over weight shuffling (median benefit, 0.00210; 28 of 34
patients; \(q=8.6\times10^{-4}\)), but the corresponding rank-distribution
benefit was not significant (0.00013; 20 of 34; \(q=0.305\)). Neither primary
endpoint improved jointly over the no-history, single-path or mode-shuffled
controls (Fig. 6C). The complete comparison gate therefore failed.

### The imposed graph and path modes were not necessary for free-running output

Removing the patient-specific graph produced only small and inconsistent
changes in participation MAE (median benefit of the intact model, 0.00095; 20
of 34 patients; \(q=0.790\)) and rank-distribution distance (0.00007; 19 of
34; \(q=0.790\)). Collapsing the two paths also failed both endpoints
(participation, −0.00030; 12 of 34; \(q=0.818\); rank distribution, 0.00001;
17 of 34; \(q=0.818\)). Removing inhibition, either direction or the dominant
mode likewise did not produce a consistent impairment (Fig. 6D). The
development-excluded 31-patient sensitivity analysis gave the same negative
decision for both the comparison and structural-necessity gates.

### Event prefixes did not identify a persistent path-direction state

The latent component posterior remained close to uniform throughout the
event. Median normalized entropy decreased only from 0.999 at event onset to
0.975 after the complete event had been observed, whereas the maximum
component probability increased from 0.264 to 0.332. The inferred probability
of forward propagation remained near 0.50, and occupancy of the first path
remained near 0.53 (Fig. 6E). Thus, although the recurrent state accumulated
excitation and inhibition, it did not resolve a stable patient-specific path
or direction.

The joint interictal gate failed and the preregistered seizure target was
therefore not accessed (Fig. 6F). This result does not test whether interictal
rank fields and early-ictal energy fields share information. It instead shows
that the present teacher-forced, event-persistent path-mode architecture is
not a sufficient generative bridge for that question.

## Claim boundary

### 允许写

- 真实 interictal rank prefix 含有可学习的局部 next-set 统计。
- 当前 structured path-mode graph RNN 在 teacher-forced NLL 上优于多个对照。
- 这些局部收益没有转化为优于简单对照的完整触点参与和 rank distributions。
- 当前路径图、多路径、方向与共享抑制没有被自由生成终点识别为必要结构。
- clinical-onset 发作期目标未读取；当前结果是纯间期的 34 人 bounded negative。

### 不允许写

- 当前模型已复现间期传播动力学。
- 模型内部自然恢复了 A/B 或稳定的正反传播状态。
- 本结果否定间期事件与发作早期能量场共享病理骨架。
- 当前模型完成了发作预测、early-warning 或 prospective forecasting。
- 当前结构是经过严格矩阵分解约束的 algebraic low-rank RNN。

## 后续模型边界

本模型不再重开 hidden size、学习率、seed 或 K 的优化。下一版已经改成不同的科学对象：
从间期 rank prefix 反推一个患者特异、近似对称的轴向 scaffold，以事件第一 rank set
表示 source，并用单一 propagation state 与 scalar STOP 预测下一触点及吸收式
first-arrival distribution。所有跨触点信息必须通过同一个对称图算子，不再设置
event-persistent path identity，也不以无条件自由生成分布作为主要阻断门。

新模型不再重复 SNN 参数恢复。三位 geometry-complete development subjects 用于有限
objective 选择；22 位 development-excluded geometry-complete 患者承担 physical-axis
formal，31 人全队列只作 coordinate-free sequence sensitivity，不为缺坐标患者创造
latent-axis fallback。跨状态 primary 恢复为 frozen interictal rollout 对既有
clinical-onset `[0,10] s`、`1–150 Hz` early-ictal energy field 的预测，dynamic
recruitment rank 只作 secondary。详细合同见
`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-07-26-topic5-symmetric-axis-propagation-state-rnn-v2_2.md`。
