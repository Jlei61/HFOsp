# Supplementary figure legends S2–S8

**Supplementary Fig. 2 | Correspondence between HFO burden and clinical SOZ before and after group-event filtering.** **A,** Patient-level ROC curves for contact HFO burden within group-event windows in Yuquan (n=20) and Epilepsiae (n=15). Grey lines show patients; coloured lines and shading show cohort means and s.e.m.; insets show patient AUCs, with means of 0.873 and 0.955. **B,** Paired AUCs for all HFO detections (Raw) and HFO within group-event windows (Synchronized), with cohort means of 0.837 to 0.873 and 0.939 to 0.955, respectively. Lines join paired patients; bars and black lines show means. Two-sided paired Wilcoxon tests; * P<0.05; n.s., P≥0.05.

**Supplementary Fig. 3 | Model-order scanning of HFO recruitment sequences.** **A,** Selected K among 40 patients evaluated over K=2–10; 34/40 selected K=2. **B,** Silhouette differences relative to K=2; grey lines show patients, and the blue line and shading show the median and IQR. **C,** Percentage passing both median between-seed AMI≥0.70 and minimum cluster fraction≥0.10. K was selected by the highest median silhouette among eligible solutions. **D,** Recruitment-rank maps for E11 (K=4), E19 (K=4) and Y10 (K=6), with 2,500 HFO group events per patient. Colour denotes recruitment order, grey non-participation and hatched gaps template boundaries. Right profiles show contact-wise mean rank ±1 s.d. in the same contact order.

**Supplementary Fig. 4 | Directional separation and cross-recording-block reproducibility of HFO propagation templates.** **A,** TA–TB 3D axis-angle distributions for Rank SEQ (blue) and Spatial TRAJ. (orange) in 28 patients. Diamonds and arcs show mean ± s.d.: 101.3° ± 45.0° and 134.6° ± 34.4°, respectively; 180° indicates reverse directions. **B,** Alternating-recording-block cross-validation. Held-out HFO events were assigned to frozen training templates using recruitment ranks alone, then scored by the signed cosine between their direction and the assigned template axis, with equal weighting across folds and templates. The median score increased from 0.435 to 0.479; 22/26 patients improved. Points and lines indicate paired patients; boxes and black lines show IQRs and medians. **C,** Directions were shuffled within held-out recording blocks while training templates and event assignments remained fixed. Open and filled points show each patient's null median and observed score. **D,** Clinical SOZ-contact compactness: SOZ root-mean-square (RMS) radius divided by the median radius of 20,000 equally sized random contact subsets from the same patient (Yuquan, n=15; Epilepsiae, n=14). The dashed line marks 1; filled points indicate patient-level permutation P<0.05; horizontal and vertical lines show medians and IQRs. B/C, one-sided paired Wilcoxon tests; D, one-sided Wilcoxon tests of log2 ratios against zero; *** P<0.001.

**Supplementary Fig. 5 | Interictal HFO propagation in patients from both cohorts.** **A,** Yuquan patient Y9. **B,** Epilepsiae patient E15. The upper and lower rows of each panel show a TA and a TB event, respectively, at 24-ms intervals from 0 to 96 ms. Colour shows spatially smoothed HFO envelopes normalized within each event. Filled and open circles denote participating and non-participating contacts. Red and blue arrows show the TA and TB template spatial trajectories, respectively, from early to late recruitment rank. Time is relative to the earliest spectrogram centroid; x follows the patient’s TA propagation axis.

**Supplementary Fig. 6 | Early-seizure spectral features and frequency-band selection.** **A,** Spectral phenotypes across 324 seizures from 23 patients: Broadband, 124 (38.3%); Gamma, 76 (23.5%); low-frequency only, 7 (2.2%); and other, 117 (36.1%). The 1–150 Hz and 30–80 Hz ranges indicate the respective early-seizure energy readout bands for Broadband and Gamma. **B,** Within-patient distribution across all 17 classified seizures of E20: Broadband, 6 (35.3%); Gamma, 6 (35.3%); and other, 5 (29.4%). Broadband and Gamma are the two most frequent categories in this patient, together accounting for 70.6%, illustrating their coexistence within one patient. **C, D,** EEG and representative-contact spectrograms for Yuquan Y15/SZ3 (Broadband; D3) and Epilepsiae E20/SZ8 (Gamma; HRB1). **E,** Power gains at the same contacts in the 1–30, 30–80, 80–150 and 1–150 Hz bands, smoothed over 2 s. Blue and green curves represent Y15/SZ3 (Broadband) and E20/SZ8 (Gamma), respectively, with a legend in the upper-left corner of each subplot. Time is relative to clinical onset. Pale blue and pale red shading mark the −120 to −90 s baseline and 0–10 s early window, respectively. Power gains are expressed in dB relative to baseline.
