# Supplementary video legends

## Supplementary Video 1

**患者间期 HFO 的空间传播。** E10（视频中标为 E1146）的 TA、TB 代表事件，自左至右为触点时频谱、动态 HFO 包络场及固定模板 rank 场。动态场按各事件完整显示窗内参与触点包络的第 99 百分位归一化；时间相对各事件最早有效时频质心。显示 −8 至 50 ms，步长 2 ms，以 12.5 帧/s 播放。补充图 S5 展示另外两位患者的传播示例。

**Supplementary Video 1 | Spatial propagation of interictal HFO activity.** Representative TA and TB events from E10 (labelled E1146 in the video). From left to right: contact spectrograms, evolving HFO-envelope fields and fixed template-rank fields. Envelopes are normalized to each event’s participating-contact 99th percentile over the complete display window. Time is relative to each event’s earliest valid spectrogram centroid. The video covers −8 to 50 ms in 2-ms steps and plays at 12.5 frames/s. Selected time points are shown in Supplementary Fig. 5C/D.

## Supplementary Video 2

**不同拟合配置中的模型间期传播。** 上、下排分别为较高与较低训练误差配置，各展示 MTA/MTB 触点包络及空间活动。白圈为 core，浅蓝符号为虚拟触点；包络与空间场色标分别为 0–1 和 0–75 个 E 细胞/mm²/2 ms。各事件显示完整 250 ms 窗，时间相对最早有效质心，步长 2 ms，以 20 帧/s 播放。静态时刻见补充图 S8。

**Supplementary Video 2 | Model interictal propagation across fitting configurations.** Upper and lower rows show configurations with higher and lower training error, respectively, each with MTA/MTB contact envelopes and evolving spatial activity. White outlines mark cores and cyan symbols virtual contacts. Envelope and spatial scales are 0–1 and 0–75 E cells/mm²/2 ms, respectively. Each event retains its complete 250-ms window, with time relative to its earliest valid centroid. Frames advance by 2 ms and play at 20 frames/s. Selected time points are shown in Supplementary Fig. 8.

## Supplementary Video 4

**E10/SZ3 围发作期能量场与间期传播模板。** 左侧为固定 TA 传播场，右侧为 1–150 Hz 滑窗能量场，下方为 $Q=\max(|q_{TA}|,|q_{TB}|)$，红、蓝点分别表示 TA、TB 占优。覆盖临床起始前 120 s 至后 20 s，每窗 10 s、步长 2 s。功率以 EEG 起始前 −120 至 −90 s 为基线进行稳健标准化，色标固定为 0–5；以 6.25 帧/s 播放。

**Supplementary Video 4 | Peri-onset energy-field evolution in E10/SZ3.** Left, fixed interictal TA propagation field; right, sliding-window 1–150 Hz energy field; bottom, $Q=\max(|q_{TA}|,|q_{TB}|)$, with red and blue indicating TA and TB dominance. Windows span −120 to 20 s relative to clinical onset, with 10-s duration and 2-s steps. Power is robustly standardized to the −120 to −90 s baseline relative to EEG onset, with a fixed colour scale of 0–5. Playback is 6.25 frames/s.
